FlutterSailCode Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

FlutterSailCode is a sail aerodynamics application developed by Robert Spall. This policy applies to the iPhone, iPad, and Android versions of the app.

Information collected

FlutterSailCode does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, location information, usage analytics, or simulation results to the developer or to third parties. No account or sign-in is required.

The app does not include advertising, tracking tools, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Calculations and settings

Sail geometry, wind angles, trim settings, and other simulation inputs are processed locally on your device. Calculations and plots do not require an internet connection. The current version keeps simulation inputs and results in working memory and does not provide a saved simulation history or cloud synchronization.

Device access

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. If you choose Copy link in the user manual, the app writes that reference link to your device's clipboard. It does not read your clipboard contents.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not sell or share data from the app. Because the app does not transmit user data to the developer, there is no developer-held app data or user account to retain or delete. Uninstalling the app removes its local installation and associated app storage, subject to your device's backup and operating-system settings.

Support inquiries

If you voluntarily contact the developer, your message and contact details will be used to respond to your inquiry. Support correspondence will be retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry or meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting the address below, subject to those obligations.

Apple, Google, and external websites

Apple and Google may process information independently when you use their stores, operating systems, backup services, or optional diagnostic services. Their own privacy policies govern that processing.

The user manual includes references to external websites. If you choose to visit one using your browser, that website's privacy policy applies.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from users of any age, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or its privacy practices. The updated policy will show a revised effective date.
